Do I need an appointment? +
Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend booking in advance — especially on weekends — to ensure you get your preferred time slot and stylist. Call us or WhatsApp to book.
How long does a keratin treatment take? +
A full keratin smoothing treatment typically takes 2–3 hours depending on hair length and thickness. We recommend setting aside half a day so there's no rush. Results last 3–5 months.
What hair colour brands do you use? +
We use only premium professional brands known for long-lasting, vibrant colour with minimal damage. Our colourists will recommend the best formula for your hair type during the consultation.
Do you offer bridal hair packages? +
Absolutely! We offer comprehensive bridal packages including a trial session, wedding day styling, and pre-bridal hair treatments. Please contact us at least 2–4 weeks in advance to schedule your trial.
Where exactly are you located? +
We are at KGF-01, Taksh Galaxy Mall, NH-08, Waghodia Road, near L&T Knowledge City, Madhavpura, Vadodara — Gujarat 390019. Ample parking is available at the mall.
What are your working hours? +
We are open daily from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM — all days of the week including Sundays and public holidays. We're here when you need us!
